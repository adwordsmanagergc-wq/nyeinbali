New Year's Eve dinners in Bali 2026

A resort gala or long NYE dinner is the most comfortable way to see in the new year in Bali: no traffic once you're there, a guaranteed table and, at most beachfront and clifftop resorts, fireworks at midnight. Expect most prices to be quoted "++", with about 21% tax and service on top.

Booking tips

  • Book by November. The best tables and early-bird deals go first.
  • Check whether the price includes drinks. Free-flow packages are often good value.
  • Ask whether the venue has its own fireworks or just views of others.
  • Staying at the resort? In-house guests often get priority or better rates.
