The Westin Nusa Dua Celestial Tide NYE

A big, family-friendly resort gala followed by a beach party for the grown-ups.

What's included

  • Gala dinner with lobster and seafood
  • Live entertainment and midnight countdown
  • Beachside Bash with live DJ at IKAN
  • Indian NYE dinner at Ganesha Elite (from IDR 800,000++)

About The Westin Nusa Dua Celestial Tide NYE

The Westin Nusa Dua Celestial Tide NYE is at Mangupura Hall & IKAN, The Westin Resort Nusa Dua in Nusa Dua (Nusa Dua). It's one of the resort galas, dinners, family-friendly options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Bali. Fireworks: Nusa Dua beach displays. Age: All ages.

Price: IDR 2,300,000++ adults (about $185 AUD); kids IDR 1,150,000++ (2025 prices, 2026 TBC). "++" means tax and service (usually 21%) are added; AUD figures are approximate.
