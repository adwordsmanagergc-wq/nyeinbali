Jumeirah Bali NYE at Akasa

A palace-style clifftop resort with a formal set menu at Akasa and a family buffet, both ending with ocean fireworks.

What's included

  • Akasa set menu with sunset DJ, dance performance and live band
  • Family buffet at Segaran Dining Terrace
  • Wine or champagne pairings available
  • Fireworks over the Indian Ocean at midnight
  • Formal dress at Akasa

About Jumeirah Bali NYE at Akasa

Jumeirah Bali NYE at Akasa is at Akasa & Segaran, Jumeirah Bali in Pecatu (Uluwatu & Bukit). It's one of the fine dining, dinners, family-friendly, clifftop options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Bali. Fireworks: Midnight fireworks on site. Age: All ages (buffet); check for Akasa.

Price: Buffet IDR 1,950,000++ (kids 5–11 IDR 975,000++); Akasa set menu IDR 2,600,000++ (2025 prices, 2026 TBC). "++" means tax and service (usually 21%) are added.
