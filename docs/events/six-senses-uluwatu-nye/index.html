New Year's Eve 2026 · Pecatu, Uluwatu & Bukit

Six Senses Uluwatu NYE Dinners

Two clifftop dinners to choose from, then a countdown with fire dance and lasers above the Indian Ocean.

What's included

  • Rocka Final Feast: wood-fired roasts, Jimbaran lobster and Japanese stations
  • Watu Steakhouse: oysters, lobster ravioli, wagyu and free-flow champagne with live jazz
  • Sunset at Cliff Bar (min spend)
  • Countdown with lasers, fire dance and midnight toasts

About Six Senses Uluwatu NYE Dinners

Six Senses Uluwatu NYE Dinners is at Rocka & Watu Steakhouse, Six Senses Uluwatu in Pecatu (Uluwatu & Bukit). It's one of the fine dining, dinners, clifftop options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Bali. Fireworks: Clifftop views. Age: Check with venue.

Price: Rocka from IDR 2,300,000++ (about $185 AUD); Watu jazz and champagne from IDR 4,600,000++ (2025 prices, 2026 TBC). "++" means tax and service (usually 21%) are added; AUD figures are approximate.
