Hotel Indigo Seminyak NYE Dinner

Two good-value NYE dinners on Seminyak Beach with half-price kids, ideal for families staying in town.

What's included

  • Five-course beachfront dinner at SugarSand, or international buffet at Makase
  • Welcome drink and soft drink
  • Live DJ or band, hula and fire dance
  • Kids' and vegetarian menus
  • Tropical countdown celebration

About Hotel Indigo Seminyak NYE Dinner

Hotel Indigo Seminyak NYE Dinner is at SugarSand & Makase, Hotel Indigo Bali Seminyak Beach in Seminyak (Seminyak). It's one of the dinners, family-friendly, up to idr 1.5 million, beach clubs options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Bali. Fireworks: Seminyak Beach displays. Age: All ages.

Price: IDR 1,300,000++; kids 6–11 half price (2025 price, 2026 TBC). "++" means tax and service (usually 21%) are added.
