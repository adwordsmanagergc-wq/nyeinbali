About Bvlgari Resort Bali: Evening Under the Stars

Bvlgari Resort Bali: Evening Under the Stars is at The Pavilion, Bvlgari Resort Bali in Pecatu (Uluwatu & Bukit). It's one of the fine dining, clifftop, resort galas options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Bali. Fireworks: Clifftop views. Age: Check with venue.

Price: IDR 8,000,000++; Il Ristorante seven-course with wine IDR 10,000,000++ (2025 prices, 2026 TBC). "++" means tax and service (usually 21%) are added.
