New Year's Eve 2026 · Ungasan, Uluwatu & Bukit

Karma Beach Seaside NYE

A private-cove beach party with bonfires and a magician for the kids, reached by funicular from the clifftop.

What's included

  • International buffet on a private beach
  • DJ sets, magician and kids' entertainment
  • Beach bonfires
  • Fireworks at midnight
  • Clifftop Venetian masquerade gala at di Mare as an alternative

About Karma Beach Seaside NYE

Karma Beach Seaside NYE is at Karma Beach, Karma Kandara in Ungasan (Uluwatu & Bukit). It's one of the dinners, beach clubs, family-friendly options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Bali. Fireworks: Midnight fireworks on site. Age: All ages.

Price: IDR 2,550,000++ non-members (about $205 AUD); kids 9 and under IDR 820,000++ (2025 prices, 2026 TBC). "++" means tax and service (usually 21%) are added; AUD figures are approximate.
