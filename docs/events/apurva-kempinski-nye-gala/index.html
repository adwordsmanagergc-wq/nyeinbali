New Year's Eve 2026 · Nusa Dua, Nusa Dua

The Apurva Kempinski Grandeur Gala

A theatrical gala in one of Bali's grandest open-air lobbies, or free-flow cocktails on the rooftop.

What's included

  • Gala dinner in the open-air Pendopo with performances from across Indonesia
  • Glass of champagne
  • L'Atelier rooftop party with free-flow cocktails, wine and canapés
  • Fireworks viewing
  • Other dinners: Koral aquarium degustation, Izakaya by Oku, Reef Beach Club BBQ

About The Apurva Kempinski Grandeur Gala

The Apurva Kempinski Grandeur Gala is at Pendopo Lobby, The Apurva Kempinski Bali in Nusa Dua (Nusa Dua). It's one of the resort galas, fine dining, family-friendly, rooftops options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Bali. Fireworks: Fireworks viewing from the rooftop. Age: All ages (gala).

Price: Rooftop party IDR 1,500,000++ (about $120 AUD); gala IDR 4,888,000++ (kids 6–12 IDR 2,700,000++) (2025 prices, 2026 TBC). "++" means tax and service (usually 21%) are added; AUD figures are approximate.
